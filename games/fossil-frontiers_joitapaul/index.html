Field protocol

  • Survey a cell to count fragments in its surrounding 3 × 3 area. Each scan marks that area and reveals its clue for your next 2 excavations.
  • Each excavation costs 1 of 19 dig charges. Empty cells use a charge too.
  • Recover all 8 fragments to win. Running out of dig charges first ends the expedition.
  • Click or tap a cell, or focus it and press Enter/Space. Arrow keys move the selected cell. Press 1 to excavate or 2 to survey.
