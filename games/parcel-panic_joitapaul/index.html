Shift guide

How to play

  1. Each parcel enters at its labeled IN tile. Route it to the OUT tile with the same label.
  2. Select a conveyor with a mouse or touch, or focus it with Tab. Click, tap, Enter, or Space to rotate it clockwise.
  3. Plan every route, then choose Run parcels. Parcels move one conveyor at a time; the direction arrow determines their next cell.
  4. Wrong destinations, empty floor, and collisions each cost one mistake. After a failed run, parcels reset so you can adjust the route.
  5. Deliver every parcel to advance through three stages. Lose all three mistakes and the shift ends. Restart resets the whole game.
