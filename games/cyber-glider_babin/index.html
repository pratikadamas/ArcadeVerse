Aerodynamic Pilot Manual

Pilot your quantum drone through neon skyscraper canyons utilizing aerodynamic physics!

  • Pitch Down / Dive: Press ↓ or hold Dive to trade altitude for airspeed.
  • Pitch Up / Climb: Press ↑ or hold Pitch Up to convert forward airspeed into vertical lift.
  • Stalling: If your forward airspeed drops too low (< 40 km/h), your wings stall and you plummet!
  • Thermal Updrafts: Fly into pulsating violet thermal air columns to ride powerful rising air currents!
  • Energy Rings: Thread through floating neon cyan rings to boost speed and score!
  • Hazards: Avoid skyscraper rooftops, radar antennas, and descending laser grids.
