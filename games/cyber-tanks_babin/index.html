Artillery Commander Briefing

Out-calculate the enemy cyber tank in turn-based ballistic warfare!

  • Elevation & Power: Set your barrel angle (0° to 90°) and charge percentage (10% to 100%).
  • Wind Vector: Shifting wind adds lateral acceleration to projectiles mid-air. Adjust your calculations each turn.
  • Destructible Hills: Projectile blasts carve dynamic craters out of the terrain canvas, collapsing elevated vantage perches.
  • Ordnance Options: Choose between direct Standard Cannons, splitting Cluster Warheads, or deep Ground Piercers.
  • AI Opponent: The enemy AI tank learns from previous missed shots and zeros in on your coordinates!
