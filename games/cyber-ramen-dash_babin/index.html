Neo-Tokyo Chef Protocol

Welcome, Cyber Chef! Cyborg patrons expect prompt, immaculate ramen bowls before their battery reserves deplete.

  • Step 1: Select the matching broth kettle requested on the customer ticket.
  • Step 2: Tap Boil Fresh Noodles to add bouncy noodles to the broth.
  • Step 3: Tap requested toppings (Chashu, Egg, Nori, Green Onion) in sequence.
  • Step 4: Tap Serve Slot 1/2/3 matching the customer order!
  • Rush Bonus: Serving fast yields extra credits and builds combo multipliers!
  • Strikes: 3 incorrect orders or expired patience meters will shut down your kitchen.
