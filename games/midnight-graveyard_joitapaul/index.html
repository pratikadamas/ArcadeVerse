How to play

The fog hides the paths ahead. Collect all three crypt keys, then find the iron gate. Ghosts roam the lanes, so keep moving.

  • WASD or Arrow keys to move
  • F toggles the lantern; its light fades as energy runs low
  • E recharges at a marked lantern shrine
  • Collect three keys, avoid ghosts, and enter the gate to escape
  • Esc pauses; R restarts the run

On touch screens, use the directional pad below the maze. Recharge at glowing shrines.
