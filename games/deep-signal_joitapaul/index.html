Field guide

How to play

  1. Move one cell at a time with the arrow keys or W, A, S, D. Touch players can use the navigation pad.
  2. Press Space or Send sonar pulse to reveal nearby terrain, hazards, and rescue beacons for 2.5 seconds. Each pulse uses one charge.
  3. Rock walls block movement. Entering a hazard costs one integrity point. Your submarine has four points of integrity.
  4. Reach all three rescue beacons to win. The mission ends if integrity reaches zero. Sonar charges are limited, so plan your route.
All locations and mission events are fictional.
