Cyber Curling Rulebook

Slide glowing quantum stones down an icy sheet into the scoring house rings!

  • Delivery: Aim your delivery trajectory line laterally and lock launch force within the sweet spot.
  • Sweeping Friction: While your stone glides down the ice sheet, vigorously tap Space or the SWEEP ICE! button to heat the ice, reducing friction and extending slide distance!
  • Scoring House: Concentric rings award points:
    • Center Button = 500 Pts
    • 4-Foot Ring = 300 Pts
    • 8-Foot Ring = 150 Pts
  • Collision Knocks: Smash into opponent stones to blast them out of the house into the backboard!
  • Ends: Play 4 ends against the AI bot. Highest aggregate score claims the championship!
