How to Play

Objective

You are the cyan mallet at the bottom. Hit the puck into the magenta goal at the top. The first side to score 7 goals wins the match.

Controls

  • Mouse: move the pointer. Your mallet follows it and stays in your half.
  • Touch: drag a finger on the table. The mallet sits just above your fingertip.
  • Keyboard: WASD or the arrow keys move the mallet.

Scoring

A goal counts when the whole puck crosses a goal line. The side that conceded serves the next puck after a short 3-2-1 countdown. The score and the result are announced in the status line under the table. Your wins and losses this session are kept in the scoreboard.

Pause and restart

  • Esc or P: pause or resume.
  • R: restart the match at 0 : 0.
  • M: sound on or off. H: open this guide.

Tips

  • A moving mallet hits much harder than a still one. Swing through the puck.
  • The puck keeps most of its speed off the walls, so bank shots work.
  • The AI predicts bounces too. Rookie reacts late, Pro reads bank shots, Master is quick and accurate.
  • If the puck rests in a corner or is pinned against the rim, a plasma pulse nudges it free.
