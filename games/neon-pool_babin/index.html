Neon Billiards Rulebook

Sink all 9 neon object balls into the 6 table pockets in as few shots as possible!

  • Aiming Cue: Move your mouse or touch drag around the cue ball to rotate the aiming guide trajectory.
  • Charge Shot: Click and drag away from the cue ball, or hold down Space / tap STRIKE BALL to charge power.
  • Cushion Rebounds: Use elastic bank bounces against table rails to curve around obstacles.
  • Fouls: Sinking the white cue ball incurs a scratch penalty and respawns it in the kitchen area.
  • Physics: Realistic 2D circle-circle momentum transfer and felt rolling deceleration.
