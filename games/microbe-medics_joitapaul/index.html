Protocol

How to play

  1. Select a barrier or cure, then choose a valid grid cell. Repeat with your remaining resources, then end the turn.
  2. After each turn, infected cells each try to infect one neighbor in up, right, down, left order. No more than two new cells are infected per turn.
  3. Barriers block infection. Cured cells are protected. Diagonal cells do not spread infection.
  4. Clear every infection, or prevent all spread for three turns, to win. The outbreak overwhelms the zone at 18 infected cells.
All outbreak organisms and events in this game are fictional.
