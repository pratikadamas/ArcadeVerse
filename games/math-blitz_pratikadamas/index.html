Rules & Controls

60 Seconds Countdown

You have exactly 60 seconds on the clock to answer 10 arithmetic challenges. The sprint completes when either the 60s timer expires or all 10 equations are answered.

Dynamically Generated Infix Equations

Every equation is algorithmically synthesized with a length of at least 6 characters using operators +, −, ×, and ÷.

Integer Precision Only

All division operations evaluate to clean integers with zero remainder. There is never any division by zero, and answers are strictly integer values.

Controls & Input

Type your answer using your physical keyboard or the on-screen arcade keypad. Press Enter or click Submit to submit immediately. The input field autofocuses automatically after every submission.

Scoring & Victory

Your final score is displayed as X / 10. Aim for 10 / 10 to achieve the Math Grandmaster title!
