Neon Alley Handbook

Welcome to Neon Bowling DX! Play authentic 10-frame championship tenpin bowling across an electrifying synthwave lane.

  • Lateral Positioning: Drag the neon ball horizontally or use ← and → arrow keys to adjust your approach angle.
  • Aim Indicator: The laser guide oscillates automatically. Tap LOCK & ROLL or press Space to lock your aiming line.
  • Power Meter: An oscillating velocity gauge determines your ball speed. Press again to release!
  • Hook & Spin: Adjust the spin slider to impart lateral spin into the oil pattern for wicked pocket hooks.
  • Pin Physics: 10-pin triangle collision simulation with rebound gutter banking.
  • Scoring: Official bowling rules with Strikes (X), Spares (/), open frames, and tenth-frame bonus balls!
