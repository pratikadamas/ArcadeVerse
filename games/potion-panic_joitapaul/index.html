How to play

Ingredients fall into their matching lanes. Select the matching cauldron before an ingredient reaches it to score; a wrong selection or missed ingredient costs one life.

  • A / 1 selects Thorn
  • S / 2 selects Moon
  • D / 3 selects Ember
  • ← → switch between cauldrons

On a touch screen, tap a cauldron to select it.

Choose a cauldron, then catch the matching ingredient.
