How to Play & Controls (PA Review)

  • Movement: Use Left Arrow / Right Arrow or A / D keys.
  • Laser Cannon: Press Spacebar or tap the FIRE button.
  • Defense Bunkers: Take cover behind the 3 destructible green energy bunkers. They degrade as lasers strike them.
  • Mystery Mothership: High-value UFOs fly across the top for 100-300 bonus points.
  • Invasion Loss: If any alien reaches the bottom bunker line, the planetary defense fails immediately.
