How to Play

Objective

Rotate the garden tiles until the seed connects to every terminal flower.

Controls

Mouse / touch: Select and rotate a tile.

Arrow keys / WASD: Move the selection.

Enter / Space: Rotate the selected tile.

Scoring

Score increases with level completion. Faster solves and fewer moves earn larger bonuses.

Pause and Restart

Press P to pause and R to restart. Use the toolbar buttons as alternatives.
