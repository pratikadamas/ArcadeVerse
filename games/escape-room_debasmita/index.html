THE WITCH'S CRYPT: SURVIVAL & ESCAPE DOSSIER

You are trapped in the sunken crypt beneath the ancestral chapel. Three ancient wards bar the Iron Gate: corroded chains, a gilded keyhole, and an arcane eye sigil. Solve the occult puzzles and unseal the gate before midnight!

1. INVESTIGATE CRYPT HOTSPOTS

Move your reticle over the room to inspect the Bookshelf, Cauldron, Pedestal, Braziers, Plant Pot, and Sarcophagus Chest.

2. EQUIP INVENTORY ITEMS

Click any item in your bottom inventory to hold it. When selected, click room objects or puzzles to use it.

3. BREW THE ACIDIC ELIXIR

Gather Mandrake Root from the plant pot, smoldering Cursed Ash from the braziers using your Silver Dagger, and Spider Venom. Brew them in the cauldron to melt the gate chains!

4. BREAK SEALS & ESCAPE

Align the celestial altar dials (Moon, Pumpkin, Skull) to obtain keys, unseal the Bone Chest for the Eye Talisman, and unlock all 3 gate wards to break free!

YOU ESCAPED!

The heavy iron gate swings open, and the cool autumn night air greets your escape from the witch's crypt!
