Laser Calibration Protocol

Guide the emitter's neon photon beam across the optical testing chamber to illuminate all receiving target nodes.

  • Select Prism Tool: Choose between diagonal redirect mirrors (/ or \), beam splitters (+), or eraser.
  • Place & Rotate: Click or tap an empty cell on the grid to deploy the active prism. Click an existing prism to rotate its orientation.
  • Laser Physics: The laser shoots from emitter nodes and bounces according to Snell's law of reflection. Splitters split incoming photons orthogonally.
  • Avoid Absorbers: Dark obsidian blocks completely extinguish light beams on collision.
  • Goal: Energize every power node on the grid simultaneously to unlock the next sector.
