How to Play

  • Goal: Hop forward as far as you can. Every new row earns points, scaled by your multiplier.
  • Keyboard: Arrow keys or W A S D hop one cell. A key pressed during a hop is buffered for the next one.
  • Touch: Swipe up, down, left or right to hop. A tap hops forward.
  • Freeways: Cars cross each lane at different speeds. Touching one ends the run.
  • Magnetic trains: A rail lane flashes red and beeps, then a very fast train sweeps through. Do not be on it.
  • Laser rivers: Never land on the laser. Hop onto floating energy logs and ride them. Being carried off the edge of the screen is fatal too.
  • Pylons: Neon pylons on safe strips block your way. Hop around them.
  • Quantum bits: Collect glowing bits for bonus points. Each bit raises your score multiplier.
  • Scanner drone: Stall for too long and a scanner drone sweeps up from behind. Keep moving forward.
  • Difficulty: The farther you go, the faster and denser the lanes get.
  • Menu keys: P or Esc pauses, H opens this guide, Enter starts or restarts.

Swipe or use arrow keys / WASD. Tap to hop forward.
