Neon Klondike Protocol

Clear the table by stacking all cards into the four foundation piles from Ace to King by suit!

  • Tableau Stacking: Stack cards in descending rank with alternating neon colors (Red suits on Cyan suits, and vice versa).
  • Foundations: Build up each suit pile starting from Ace through King.
  • Stock & Waste: Tap the Stock deck on the top left to cycle new cards to the Waste pile.
  • Undo & Hints: Use the unlimited Undo button to revert moves, or tap Hint for available legal maneuvers.
  • Auto-Complete: Once all tableau cards are face-up, tap Auto Finish to route them into foundations instantly!
