DISPATCH NOTE / LINE 13

How to play

Two golden switches are misaligned. Dispatch the train, then switch each one to keep its incoming rail connected to the station route before the engine arrives.

  • Click or tap a switch to rotate it; selected switches have a gold outline
  • Arrow keys select a board tile; Space rotates a switch
  • Enter dispatches the train; P pauses and R retries
  • Every switch change counts as one move. Route around broken rails to reach the station

You can change a switch while the train is moving. A wrong connection or broken rail derails the run.
