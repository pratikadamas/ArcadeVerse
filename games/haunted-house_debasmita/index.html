CASE DOSSIER #1898: BLACKWOOD MANOR

"Midnight, October 31. You arrive stranded outside the foreboding gates of Blackwood Manor. Inside lies an unbroken occult curse from 1898. To escape alive, you must investigate the rooms, solve ancient mechanisms, and exorcise the cellar altar before the darkness consumes your mind."

1. INVESTIGATE ROOMS

Move your mouse around each room. The dynamic crosshair reticle illuminates clues, keys, and hidden secret compartments.

2. EQUIP & USE ITEMS

Click any item in your bottom inventory pockets to hold it. When an item is active, clicking compatible doors, safes, or altars will use it.

3. MANAGE FLASHLIGHT & SANITY

Press [F] to toggle your flashlight. Remaining in pitch blackness steadily erodes your mental sanity. If sanity hits 0, madness takes over.

4. SURVIVE PARANORMAL ATTACKS

When the screen turns blood-red and whisper voices surge, press [H] or click HIDE immediately to conceal yourself in the wardrobe.
