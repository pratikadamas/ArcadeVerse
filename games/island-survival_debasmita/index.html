THE CURSED ATOLL: SURVIVAL & ESCAPE DOSSIER

You washed ashore on the uncharted and cursed Devil's Atoll following a harrowing crash. The dense mangrove jungle harbors ancient horrors that awaken when the sun sets. Maintain your vitals, build campfires to stay alive through the night, and plan your escape!

1. EXPLORE & GATHER

Move with WASD, Arrow Keys, or the Touch D-Pad. Walk up to trees, rocks, bushes, or wreckage and press [SPACE] or tap [ACT] to harvest supplies.

2. SURVIVE THE NIGHTMARE NIGHT

When night falls, terrifying Shadow Stalkers emerge from the jungle! Build a Campfire or craft a Survival Torch via [C]. Firelight is your ONLY shield against the creatures.

3. MONITOR VITALS & REST

Keep Health, Hunger, Thirst, and Energy above zero. Forage berries and coconuts (press [E] to eat), drink from the spring, and rest near a campfire with [R].

4. CRAFT & ESCAPE

Open Crafting with [C]. Forge Stone Axes and Spears. Escape by building a Sea Raft at the coast OR by salvaging the plane's battery to power the Cliff SOS Beacon!
