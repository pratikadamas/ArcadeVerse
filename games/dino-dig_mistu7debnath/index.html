HOW TO PLAY DINO DIG

Master your archaeological tools and lead your expedition to paleontological glory.

1

Choose Tool

Pick Brush [1] for delicate soft soil, Shovel [2] for deep earth, Pickaxe [3] for boulders, or Radar [4] to ping fossil echoes.

2

Click to Excavate

Click any soil tile. Your archaeologist will walk over and actively dig, brush, or strike the terrain.

3

Find 2 Fossils

Every site hides 2 dinosaur bones. Watch for amber gems and canteens that restore vitality!

4

Assemble Skeletons

Visit the Museum to mount bones onto Triceratops, Stegosaurus, T-Rex, and Brachiosaurus skeletons!
