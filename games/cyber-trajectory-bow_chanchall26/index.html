How to Play

Controls and Rules

  • Aim: press anywhere on the field and drag away from where you want to shoot, like a slingshot. The bow turns and the string draws back.
  • Power: the farther you pull, the faster the arrow. Pull is capped at a limit. Release to fire. A very short pull cancels the shot.
  • Preview: the dotted curve is the real flight path using the same physics, including wind and gravity. It stops where the arrow first touches a wall, floor or barrier.
  • Goal: destroy every node with the arrows you have. Run out with nodes left and the stage fails.
  • Keys: P or Esc pause, R restart stage, M sound, H this screen. Right-click cancels an aim. Touch works the same way.

Nodes

  • Standard - one hit.
  • Moving - slides or orbits on a path. Lead your shot.
  • Armored - takes several hits. Pips show remaining hit points and cracks grow. Hits bounce the arrow off.
  • Explosive - blasts every node nearby when destroyed. Blasts chain into other explosives. Armor takes 2 damage.

Consecutive successful shots build a streak that multiplies points (+25% per link, up to x3). A shot that hits nothing resets it. Chain-reaction kills score extra. Finish with arrows to spare and good accuracy for up to three stars.

Wind and Gravity

From stage 5 the wind pushes arrows sideways or up and down. The HUD arrow shows its direction and strength. Later stages change gravity (shown as a multiplier) and may shift both between shots. The preview line always accounts for them.
