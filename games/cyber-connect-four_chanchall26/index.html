How to Play

Objective

Take turns dropping discs into a 7 by 6 grid. Connect four of your discs in a row, horizontally, vertically or diagonally, before your rival does. If all 42 slots fill up with no four in a row, the game is a draw. Pink discs carry a ring and cyan discs carry a diamond, so the two sides never rely on color alone.

Controls

  • Keyboard: Left and Right move the column cursor, Enter or Space drops a disc, and 1 to 7 drops straight into that column.
  • Mouse: move over the board to preview the column and the landing slot, then click to drop.
  • Touch: tap a column, or touch and slide to choose a column and lift your finger to drop.
  • R restarts, P or Esc pauses, M mutes sound, H opens this guide.

Modes and difficulty

  • Player vs AI: you play pink, the AI plays cyan. Easy looks 2 moves ahead, Medium 4 and Hard 6.
  • Two players: share one screen. Pink is Player 1, cyan is Player 2.
  • The AI uses minimax search with alpha-beta pruning. It always takes a win it can see, blocks yours, and scores the rest by counting open threats in every group of four plus control of the centre columns. Moves are tried from the centre outward.
  • No move is accepted while a disc is falling or while the AI is thinking.

Scoring

  • Every finished game adds one to the winner's session counter, or one to Draws. Counters are kept separately for Player vs AI and Two players.
  • The counters last for this browser tab session. Use Reset session score in the menu to clear them.
  • After a finished game, the player who moved second starts the next one.

Restart and pause

  • Restart (R or the restart button) clears the board. Pause (P, Esc or the pause button) freezes the game, including the AI, until you resume.
  • The menu button lets you switch mode or difficulty at any time.
