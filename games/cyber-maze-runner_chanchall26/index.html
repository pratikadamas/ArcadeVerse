How to Play

Goal

Collect every energy node (small dots) to clear the level. Each level the drones get faster and the EMP effect gets shorter.

Controls

  • WASD or arrow keys: choose a direction. You keep moving and turn at the next opening.
  • Touch: swipe on the board in the direction you want to go.
  • P or Esc: pause. M: sound on or off.

Overdrive EMP nodes

The four large glowing nodes at the corners turn every drone vulnerable for a few seconds. Touch a vulnerable drone to destroy it for 200, 400, 800 and 1600 points in a row. A destroyed drone returns to its pen and comes back. When they flash, the effect is about to end.

The drones

  • Blitz (red) heads straight for your tile.
  • Phantom (magenta) aims four tiles ahead of where you are facing, to ambush you.
  • Vector (cyan) flanks: it aims at the point mirrored through Blitz and a spot two tiles ahead of you.
  • Drifter (orange) chases from far away but retreats to its corner when it gets close.

Drones alternate between scatter (they head for their own corners) and chase. They never turn back on themselves except when the mode changes.

Scoring

  • Energy node: 10. EMP node: 50.
  • Vulnerable drone: 200, 400, 800, 1600.
  • Extra life at every 10,000 points. You start with 3 lives.

Arrow keys or WASD to steer. Swipe on touch screens.
