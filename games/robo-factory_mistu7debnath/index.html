PRODUCTION PRINCIPLES

1. Connect Dispensers to Cargo Bays: Raw materials emerge continuously from green dispensers. Lay connected conveyor belts to route them into processing machines and onward to the delivery bays.

2. Smart Machine Feeding: Machines take raw materials from any conveyor pointing into them, and eject finished goods along their active output arrow!

3. Instant Start & Zero Lag: When you click START PRODUCTION, materials begin moving immediately. You can pause or fast-forward at 2X speed anytime.
