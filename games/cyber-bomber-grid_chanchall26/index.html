How to Play

Objective

Destroy all three bots with your charges and stay alive. Clear the round to move to the next one. Lose all lives and the run ends.

Controls

  • WASD or the arrow keys: move one tile at a time along the grid.
  • Space: place a charge on your tile. You can walk off it, but not back onto it.
  • Touch or mouse: use the pad for movement and the bomb button to place a charge.
  • P or Esc: pause or resume. R: restart the run. M: sound on or off. H: this screen.

Charges and blasts

  • A charge explodes 2.5 seconds after it is placed, in a plus shape.
  • Pillars (grey, riveted) stop a blast completely and are never destroyed.
  • A block (purple, hatched) is destroyed by the blast and also stops it, so nothing behind it is hit.
  • A blast that reaches another charge sets it off at once, so charges placed in a row chain together.
  • Blast cells stay deadly for about half a second. They also destroy upgrades lying in them.
  • Your own charges can kill you. Plan your escape before you plant.

Upgrades

Some destroyed blocks drop an upgrade. Walk onto it to collect it. Bots collect them too.

  • Blast range: each arm of the blast reaches one more tile (up to 7).
  • Charge capacity: carry one more live charge at a time (up to 5).
  • Speed: move faster (up to 4 levels).

The bots

  • Viper (pink, horns): the hunter. Heads for you and digs through blocks that are in its way.
  • Digger (amber, drill): the block-breaker. Chases upgrades and clears blocks, and attacks if you are in its line.
  • Warden (cyan, ring): the cautious one. Keeps its distance, avoids your firing lines and only plants when the escape is short.

All bots search the grid for the nearest target and for a tile outside every pending blast (including chains). Before planting they check that they can get clear, and they steer around blasts they can see coming. A bot can still be boxed in by charges that go down after it plants, and then it is caught in its own blast.

Scoring

  • Block destroyed by you: 10. Upgrade collected: 50.
  • Bot destroyed by your blast: 300.
  • Chain bonus: 50 for every extra charge your blast sets off.
  • Round clear: 500 plus 5 for every second left on the clock.

Rounds, lives and sudden death

  • You have 3 lives. If you are caught, you lose one and the round restarts with a fresh arena.
  • Each round lasts 100 seconds. When the clock hits zero the outer tiles start turning into solid walls, spiralling inward. Warning flashes mark the next tiles. Anyone on a closing tile is crushed.
  • Each cleared round makes the bots faster.
