How to Play

1. Pick Ingredients

Click ingredients from your inventory panel (left). Up to 3 at a time go into the cauldron slots.

2. Brew

Press "Brew Potion". If you've used the right ingredients, you'll craft a potion! Wrong combos make mysterious smoke.

3. Fulfill Orders

Customers on the right need specific potions. Deliver them before time runs out for bonus coins!

4. Buy More Ingredients

Use earned coins in the Shop to restock. Discover all recipes to unlock Master Alchemist status!
