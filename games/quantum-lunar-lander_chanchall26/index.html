Flight Manual

Mission

Bring the descent module down onto a landing pad without breaking it. Every level uses a new procedurally generated moon surface, and the pads get smaller and the gravity gets stronger as you go.

Controls

Main thrusterW / ↑ or the THRUST button
Rotate leftA / ← or the left rotate button
Rotate rightD / → or the right rotate button
PauseP or Esc

The thruster pushes the ship the way its nose points. Rotation has inertia: a spin keeps going until you counter it, and slowly settles on its own. There is no air, so nothing slows you down except your engine.

Safe landing limits

Vertical speed3.5 m/s or less
Horizontal speed2.5 m/s or less
Tilt at touchdown12 degrees or less from upright
Landing gearBoth feet must be on the same pad

The V-SPEED, H-SPEED and TILT readouts (and the landing gear on your ship) glow green when the value is inside the limit and red when touching down right now would destroy the ship. Touching rock anywhere, or hitting a pad too fast, crashes the lander.

Fuel

Fuel is limited and only burns while the main thruster is firing. When the gauge hits zero the engine is dead and you are in free fall. Later levels start with less fuel. Fuel left over after landing becomes bonus points.

Pads and multipliers

  • 2x Wide and forgiving.
  • 3x Medium width.
  • 5x Narrow, high reward.

Scoring

Landing score = (level base + soft touch bonus) x pad multiplier. Then you add a fuel bonus for each unit of fuel left. Your best score is saved in this browser.

Lives, levels and hazards

You start with 3 ships. A crash costs one ship and you retry the same terrain. A bonus ship is awarded every 3 levels. From level 3 a quantum gravity anomaly (the violet swirl) pulls the ship toward it. The dotted line shows where you will go if you stop thrusting.
