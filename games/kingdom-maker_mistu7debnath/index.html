How to Rule Your Kingdom

1. Build Homes & Attract Citizens

Construct houses from the toolbar. Houses provide population capacity. If your kingdom has plenty of food, citizens will migrate to your settlement!

2. Cultivate Resources

Farms produce food, Sawmills supply wood, Quarries extract stone, and Mines harvest gold & stone. Resources accumulate periodically.

3. Trade & Upgrade Structures

Click on existing buildings on the map to inspect or upgrade them for +50% or +100% higher productivity! Build Markets & Trading Posts to generate gold.

4. Expand Land & Erect the Castle

Unlock outer territory parcels to expand your realm. Complete the 5 kingdom stages to construct the Royal Castle and achieve victory!
