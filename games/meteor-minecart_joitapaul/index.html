How to Play

Objective

Switch lanes, collect energy crystals, and avoid meteors for as long as possible.

Controls

Keyboard: Left/Right arrows or A/D.

Mobile: Swipe left or right.

Scoring and Hazards

Crystals add score and build a multiplier. Meteors remove one health. Three collisions end the run.

Pause, Restart, and Shield

Press P to pause, R to restart, or use Down/S for one of three limited shields.
